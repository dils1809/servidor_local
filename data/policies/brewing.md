# Brewing Guide and Ingredients

VIBBO teas are loose-leaf functional blends. Every blend is caffeine-free
except Energy & Hydration, which is built on a black tea base.

## How to brew

| Blend | Water | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Energy & Hydration | 90 to 95 C | 3 to 5 min | Contains caffeine from black tea |
| Detox | 95 to 100 C | 5 to 7 min | Longer steep brings out the fennel |
| Calm | 95 to 100 C | 5 to 8 min | Caffeine-free, good before bed |

Use roughly one heaped teaspoon (2 to 3 g) per 250 ml cup. All three blends
can be re-steeped once. They also work cold: steep in cold water in the
fridge for 6 to 8 hours.

Loose leaf needs a filter. We sell **Filter Bags** separately for this.

## Ingredients by blend

**Energy & Hydration** - black tea, hibiscus, cocoa husk, pineapple pieces,
coconut, raisins, dried black lemon.

**Detox** - fennel, mint, ginger, coriander seeds, boldo, pineapple pieces.

**Calm** - rosemary, lavender, passionflower, lemon verbena, orange peel,
ginger, cinnamon.

## Allergens and sensitivities

The blends contain no gluten, dairy, soy or added sugar. Energy & Hydration
contains **coconut** (a tree nut for labelling purposes in some countries)
and **cocoa husk**.

Caffeine is present only in Energy & Hydration, from its black tea base.

## Who should ask a doctor first

These are teas, not medicine, and we make no medical claims. Some botanicals
interact with medication or are not recommended during pregnancy or
breastfeeding. Customers who are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking prescription
medication or managing a health condition should check with their doctor
before drinking a blend regularly. Boldo, in the Detox blend, is the one most
often flagged.

Support agents must never give medical advice. Point the customer to this
list of ingredients and tell them to check with their doctor.

## Storage and shelf life

Keep the pouch closed, dry and away from direct sunlight. Best within
18 months of opening. There is no refrigeration requirement.
