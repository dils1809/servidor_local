# Shipping Policy

VIBBO ships functional teas worldwide from the United States.

## Processing time

Orders are packed within **1 to 5 business days** of being placed. Orders
placed on a weekend or a US holiday start processing the next business day.
You receive a tracking email as soon as the package leaves the warehouse.

## Delivery time

Delivery time is counted from the moment the carrier picks the package up,
not from the moment the order is placed.

| Destination | Estimated delivery |
| --- | --- |
| United States (domestic) | 3 to 7 business days |
| International | 7 to 14 business days |

These are carrier estimates, not guarantees. Weather, customs and peak
seasons can add a few days.

## Carriers

We ship with **UPS**, **FedEx** and **USPS**. The carrier is chosen at
packing time based on destination and package weight. USPS is used for
domestic orders only.

## Shipping cost

Shipping is **quoted at checkout** before you pay, based on your destination
and the weight of your order. There is no flat rate and no minimum for free
shipping.

## Customs and import duties

International customers are responsible for any import duties, taxes or
customs fees charged by their country. These are not included in the order
total and are collected by the carrier on delivery.

## Wrong or incomplete address

If the address was entered incorrectly, contact us at
**support@drinkvibbo.com** as soon as possible. Once a package has shipped
we cannot reroute it. Packages returned to us as undeliverable are refunded
minus the original shipping cost.

## Lost or delayed packages

If tracking has not moved in 7 business days, or the carrier marks the
package as delivered but you did not receive it, write to
**support@drinkvibbo.com** with your order number. We open a claim with the
carrier and either reship or refund.
