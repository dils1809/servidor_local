# Returns and Refunds Policy

We want you to actually like the tea. If you do not, we make returning it
simple: no questions, no hoops.

## Return window

You have **30 days from the delivery date** to request a return. After 30
days we can no longer accept the return, but you can still write to us if
something went wrong.

## Condition of the product

You can return a product you have already opened and tried. The only
requirement is that **at least 70% of the product remains**. This lets you
taste it properly before deciding, while preventing returns of an empty box.

Bundles and assortment boxes are returned as a whole. We do not accept
partial returns of a bundle.

## How to start a return

1. Write to **support@drinkvibbo.com** with your order number and the reason.
2. We reply with return instructions and the return address.
3. Ship the product back and send us the tracking number.

Do not ship anything back before contacting us. Returns received without
prior notice cannot be matched to an order.

## Who pays for return shipping

The customer pays return shipping, except when the return is our fault:
a wrong item, a damaged item or a defective product. In those cases we cover
the return shipping and you do not need to ship it back in some cases; a
photo may be enough.

## Refund timing

Once we receive and inspect the return, the refund is issued to the original
payment method within **5 to 7 business days**. Your bank may take a few
extra days to show it.

The original shipping cost is not refunded, except when the return is our
fault.

## Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong product, write to
**support@drinkvibbo.com** within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We reship
or refund at no cost to you.

## Cancellations

An order can be cancelled while it is still unfulfilled. Once it has
shipped, it becomes a return and the policy above applies.
